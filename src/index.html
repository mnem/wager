What this doesn't include

The calculation covers income tax for and employee National Insurance only.

It does not account for:

  • pension contributions or salary sacrifice
  • student or postgraduate loan repayments
  • benefits in kind, company cars or medical cover
  • any tax code other than the standard one
  • the marriage allowance or blind person's allowance
  • savings and dividend income, which is taxed at UK-wide rates wherever you live

Also worth knowing:

  • National Insurance is worked out annually here, but real payroll calculates it separately for each pay period. A real payslip may differ slightly.
  • The salary shown is rounded up to the nearest pound, so it always clears the take-home figure you asked for rather than falling a penny short.
